# Support & Customer Success

Support is the product's sensor network. Resolve problems quickly and kindly, and feed patterns back to the team so the same problem stops recurring.

## Support readiness before launch
Support channels (email, in-app chat, community), a ticketing tool, an SLA by severity and plan (e.g. first response in 4 business hours; Critical in 1 hour), business hours and escalation coverage, a knowledge base seeded with the top 20 expected questions, canned responses (macros), a known-issues page, and a status page link. Train the team with a product walkthrough and test tickets.

## Ticket triage
Classify each ticket: question / how-to, bug, outage, feature request, billing, account/security. Assign severity by impact and reach. Reproduce bugs and capture environment, version, steps, logs and screenshots, then escalate to engineering with a complete, reproducible report and the number of affected users. Keep the customer updated until closure.

## Escalation to engineering
Defined severities with response expectations, an on-call path for outages (see `sre-incident-manager`), a single place to file bugs with a template, and a feedback loop so support learns the fix status. Security and privacy reports go straight to `security-engineer` and `compliance-privacy-officer`.

## Communication standards
Acknowledge quickly, show empathy, restate the problem, give a clear next step and timeline, avoid jargon, own mistakes, and never blame the user. Close the loop after resolution and verify it worked. Be honest about what is unknown or not planned.

## Knowledge base
Write task-based articles (with `technical-writer`): title as the user's words, problem, steps, expected result, fallback. Review top-viewed and top-searched-no-result articles monthly. Convert repeated tickets into articles, in-product hints, or fixes.

## Customer success
Onboarding checklist and activation milestones, health scoring (usage, adoption, support load, sentiment), proactive outreach for at-risk accounts, QBRs for larger customers, renewal and expansion planning, and a structured way to capture requests tagged by customer and segment.

## Metrics
First response time, time to resolution, SLA attainment, CSAT, ticket volume per active user, contact reasons, backlog age, deflection rate (self-service), NPS, churn and retention. Review trends with product and engineering monthly and turn top contact reasons into roadmap items.
